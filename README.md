# 📊 Excel-Sales-Dashboard-Project

An interactive business intelligence dashboard built in **Microsoft Excel** tracking **TrailVista’s** revenue, gross profit, margins, and regional performance across product categories, countries, and quarters.

---

##  TrailVista Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive, business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze and track financial performance, revenue drivers, and profitability metrics for **TrailVista** (an outdoor gear and apparel brand).

### 📌 Dashboard Overview
The **TrailVista Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard** provides executive stakeholders and analysts with a centralized view of key business performance indicators (KPIs). It highlights crucial metrics such as **total revenue**, **gross profit**, **sales volumes**, and **regional performance trends** across multiple product categories and sales channels.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics Tracked

* **Total Revenue:** `$6,45,223`
* **Total Gross Profit:** `$2,96,384`
* **Gross Profit Margin:** `45.94%`
* **Total Units Sold:** `6,698`
* **Total Orders:** `2,737`
* **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** `$3,48,839`

---

##  Key Features & Visualizations

* **Executive KPI Cards:** High-level overview of **revenue**, **profit margins**, **units sold**, **orders**, and **COGS**.
* **Monthly Revenue & Profit Trends:** Line charts mapping seasonal fluctuations and financial performance across the year (**January – December**).
* **Top 5 Revenue-Generating Countries:** Bar charts displaying regional performance leaders (**United Kingdom**, **Spain**, **Italy**, **Germany**, and **France**).
* **Revenue Contribution by Channel:** Breakdown of sales performance across channels, led by **Online Store (40%)**, **Corporate (28%)**, **Retail Partners (18%)**, and **Marketplace (14%)**.
* **Region-Wise & Category Comparisons:** Doughnut charts and bar distributions showing regional breakdowns (**Central, Northern, Southern, and Western Europe**) alongside Gross Profit by Product Category (**Camping, Cycling, Fitness, Hiking, and Travel**).
* **Interactive Slicers / Filters:** Dynamic filtering options for **Product Categories**, **Countries**, and **Quarters (Q1–Q4)** to drill down into specific segments.

---

##  Tech Stack & Tools

* **Primary Tool:** Microsoft Excel (`.xlsx`)
* **Data Analysis:** Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
* **Data Visualization:** Interactive Slicers, KPI Cards, Custom Dashboard UI, and Dynamic Charts

---

##  How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository to your local machine.
2. Open the `.xlsx` workbook in **Microsoft Excel**.
3. Use the interactive slicers on the right panel (**Product Category**, **Country**, and **Quarter**) to dynamically filter the visuals and explore specific insights.
